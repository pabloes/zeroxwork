## Post 2 — el formato

The format:

- 1 to 10 pages, cover not counted
- PNG, in reading order
- Any style: ink, manga, pixel art, 3D renders
- Readable in English
- Original work

Generative AI is allowed. Just tell us how you used it: AI and non-AI entries are judged on the same criteria.

---

## Post 3 — como se entrega

How to enter:

1. Join the Discord and link your free ZEROxWORK account
2. Publish your comic as an article on zeroxwork.com
3. Share it with #ZEROxCITY
4. Reply to this thread with the link

https://discord.gg/MMXaM4VgWd

## Post 4 — el jurado

Judged on creativity (35%), storytelling (30%), visual quality (20%) and metaverse connection (15%).

Likes and reposts count for nothing.

One entry per person or team. Everything else is in the rules:
