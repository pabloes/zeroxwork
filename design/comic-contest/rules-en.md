**One title. Infinite stories.**

Create an original comic titled **ZEROxCITY**, inspired by the metaverse. Tell any story you want: science fiction, comedy, adventure, horror, fantasy, or drama.

**Organized by:** ZEROxWORK.com

**Prize:** 100 USDC

**Submission Deadline:** December 21, 2026

**Entry Fee:** Free

## 1. Theme and Creativity

- Every comic must be titled **ZEROxCITY**.
- The story must take place in or be meaningfully connected to the metaverse.
- Any genre is welcome.
- Participants have complete creative freedom to interpret ZEROxCITY however they wish.
- Stories do not need to follow any existing storyline or fictional universe.

## 2. Comic Requirements

- **Length:** Minimum 1 page, maximum 10 pages.
- An optional cover is allowed and does not count toward the page limit.
- **Accepted format:** PNG only.
- Pages must be submitted in the correct reading order.
- Comics will be published as articles on ZEROxWORK.com.
- Any artistic style is welcome, including traditional illustration, manga, digital art, pixel art, 3D renders, and more.
- Comics must be readable in English. Translations are welcome.
- All submissions must be original works.

## 3. Use of Artificial Intelligence

**Generative AI is allowed**, both for illustrations and storytelling.

Participants must disclose whether they used generative AI and briefly describe how it was used.

AI-assisted and non-AI entries will compete equally under the same judging criteria.

## 4. How to Participate

1. **Join the [official ZEROxWORK Discord server](https://discord.gg/MMXaM4VgWd) and verify your account** by registering for free at [ZEROxWORK.com](https://zeroxwork.com) and linking your Discord account.
2. Create a comic that follows the contest requirements.
3. Publish your comic as an article on ZEROxWORK.com, including all pages in PNG format.
4. Share your article on X using the hashtag **#ZEROxCITY**.
5. Reply to the official contest announcement on X with a link to your article.
6. Disclose whether generative AI was used.

**Additional Conditions:**

- Participation is completely free.
- Individuals and teams are welcome.
- Only one entry per participant or team is allowed.
- Participants must maintain their linked account and Discord membership until the contest ends.
- Contest organizers and judges are not eligible to participate.

## 5. Contest Dates

- **Start:** Immediately upon the official contest announcement.
- **Submission Deadline:** December 21, 2026, at 23:59 UTC.

Late submissions will not be accepted.

## 6. Judging Criteria

Entries will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

- **Creativity and originality — 35%**
- **Storytelling and narrative — 30%**
- **Visual quality and composition — 20%**
- **Connection to the metaverse — 15%**

The judging panel will consist of **Pablo and additional judges to be announced**.

The entry receiving the highest overall score will be declared the winner.

In the event of a tie, the entry with the highest creativity and originality score will win, followed by the storytelling score if necessary.

Judges must disclose conflicts of interest and may not evaluate entries where such conflicts exist.

## 7. Prize

### Grand Prize: 100 USDC

**One winner will receive 100 USDC.**

- The prize will be transferred to a compatible cryptocurrency wallet on the blockchain network specified by the organizer.
- Transaction fees will be covered by ZEROxWORK.
- For team submissions, a single prize payment will be made.
- The winner is responsible for any applicable taxes.
- The prize will be delivered within 14 days after the winner is notified and provides a valid wallet address.

## 9. Fair Play and Disqualification

Entries may be disqualified if they:

- Fail to comply with the contest requirements.
- Contain plagiarized material or infringe third-party intellectual property rights.
